Q65. Remove all the employees working for the IT department.


Delete
From Employees
Where department_id = (Select department_id
                       from departments
                       where department_name = 'IT');

Q66. Remove from the database all the departments loacted in Seattle.

Delete
from departments
where location_id = (Select location_id
                     from Locations
                     where city = 'Seattle');
